Rethinking SAE Evaluation: An Atomic Interpretable Unit Framework Reveals Hidden Polysemanticity and Redundancy

accepted NeurIPS 2026 · Evaluations & Datasets Track

Riteng Zhang, Xiaoqian Wang

We evaluate sparse autoencoders as systems of atomic interpretable units, examining non-redundancy, completeness, monosemanticity, dead units, and sparsity. Our framework reveals a dominant-feature failure mode: highly influential features can remain polysemantic or redundant even when conventional evaluations appear favorable.
